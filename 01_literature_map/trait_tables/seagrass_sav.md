## Temperature Relevance

- **Mechanism:** *Zostera marina* is at its southern thermal limit in NC; prolonged summer heat (>~25°C) causes physiological dieback
- **Degree-day relevance:** **Low** — thermal accumulation less relevant than acute threshold exceedance for SAV dieback
- **B1 test:** Degree-days expected to add little for seagrass compared to temperature extremes
- **Species dependency:** *Halodule wrightii* tolerates much higher temperatures — species choice changes the threshold story entirely

## Salinity Relevance

- **Mechanism:** SAV species have salinity preferences but are generally less acutely sensitive than oysters
- **Known risk:** Extreme freshening can damage beds, but salinity is secondary to temperature and light

## Dissolved Oxygen Relevance

- **Mechanism:** SAV beds are oxygen producers during daylight; hypoxia is more a consequence of SAV loss than a driver
- **Compression role:** Minimal — SAV is not directly "compressed" by DO in the same way as mobile taxa
- **Known risk:** Sediment oxygen demand and nighttime respiration are real but poorly measured

## Turbidity / Light Relevance

- **Mechanism:** PAR attenuation controls maximum colonization depth; sustained turbidity causes deep-edge bed retreat
- **Essential for SAV:** This is the primary non-thermal driver (supports B2 pathway, even if B2 is deferred for direct burial)
- **Known risk:** Biofouling on optical sensors; separating sediment, algae, and CDOM from basic NTU readings
- **Reviewer concern:** "Basic optical sensors" may not distinguish cause of light limitation

## Habitat Dependency

- **Benthic:** Yes — rooted in shallow subtidal sediments
- **Depth requirement:** Shallow — limited by light penetration
- **Substrate:** Soft sediment (sand, mud); needs stable substrate
- **Spatial fixity:** Sessile; responds to local conditions over weeks to seasons

## Degree-Day Relevance

- **Expected value:** Low — SAV dieback is more about acute threshold exceedance than cumulative thermal energy
- **B1 test:** Degree-days expected to add least for seagrass among all focal targets
