## Temperature Relevance

- **Mechanism:** Thermal stress; seasonal migration cues; metabolic rate
- **Degree-day relevance:** **High** — growth rate, nursery residency timing (supports B1)
- **Degree-day base temperature:** TODO: Literature value needed
- **Known risk:** Temperature preferences may shift with life stage and season

## Salinity Relevance

- **Mechanism:** Euryhaline species but juveniles prefer lower salinity nursery habitats
- **Known risk:** Less acutely sensitive to salinity shocks than oysters; salinity role may be secondary to DO

## Dissolved Oxygen Relevance

- **Mechanism:** Hypoxia avoidance is the primary compression driver — same squeeze mechanism as blue crab
- **Compression role:** Core — flounder redistribute from hypoxic bottom waters to shallows
- **Catchability paradox:** Same as blue crab — CPUE may spike in oxygenated refugia during hypoxic events
- **Known risk:** Bottom DO sampling gaps; nighttime hypoxia missed

## Turbidity / Light Relevance

- **Direct relevance:** Low for Southern Flounder

## Habitat Dependency

- **Benthic:** Yes — obligately benthic flatfish
- **Depth preference:** Shallow to moderate depths in estuarine waters
- **Substrate association:** Soft sediment (mud, sand)
- **Nursery dependency:** Estuarine nursery habitats critical for juvenile survival

## Degree-Day Relevance

- **Expected value:** High — growth, nursery residency, migration timing
- **B1 test:** Expected to improve prediction for finfish (similar to blue crab)
